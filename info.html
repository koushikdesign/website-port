Koushik Raju is a self-taught graphic and motion designer based in Hyderabad, India. His practice focuses on visual identity, typography, motion, and 3D design—creating work that balances clear thinking with distinctive visual character.

A Communication graduate from the University of Hyderabad, Koushik has developed his design practice through continuous self-directed learning, experimentation, and curiosity. He is interested in how ideas, stories, and systems can be translated into engaging visual experiences, and enjoys building flexible identity systems across print, digital, motion, and three-dimensional spaces.

Alongside design work, he explores 3D forms, animation, emerging creative tools, and visual experimentation. His approach is grounded in research, play, and a belief that thoughtful systems create work with lasting impact.

Available for freelance projects, collaborations, and thoughtful conversations.
