Hi, I'm Koushik.

I'm a graphic designer focused on visual identity design and motion design, based in Hyderabad, India. I studied Communication at the University of Hyderabad, where I developed an interest in how ideas, stories, and systems can be shaped through design.

I'm particularly obsessed with graphic identities: the thinking behind them, the systems that hold them together, and the countless ways they can come to life across different mediums. Outside of design work, I spend a lot of time sketching, poking around new software, experimenting with creative tools, and collecting references that catch my attention. Currently, I'm interested in visual identity, motion design, and the growing intersection between design and technology.

Available for collaborations, freelance opportunities, and good conversations.
