Jony Ive - Measuring Creativity

Client: Personal

Inspired by Jony Ive's talk at Stripe Sessions, this motion piece is a personal exploration of creativity beyond measurable success. It examines the balance between logic and intuition, questioning how ideas are shaped when they are driven by curiosity instead of metrics. Through motion, typography, and pacing, the project translates these reflections into a visual narrative.
