Zaggle x GFF 2026

Client: Zaggle

Developed the core visual identity for Zaggle's presence at the Global Fintech Fest (GFF), one of India's leading fintech events. Collaborated with the marketing, PR, events, and content teams to bring together Zaggle's brand language and GFF's visual elements, creating a cohesive and adaptable design system across event branding, social media, email campaigns, and other marketing touchpoints.
